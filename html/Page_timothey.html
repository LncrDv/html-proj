Idk what to write so here is a long recipe for a singular cookie Chocolate Chip Cookie
Ingredients

30 g all-purpose flour

1 small pinch baking soda

1 small pinch salt

15 g unsalted butter, softened

15 g brown sugar

5 g white sugar

1 tablespoon beaten egg

1–2 drops vanilla extract

20 g chocolate chips



Preparation

Step 1 Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Let the warmth slowly fill the kitchen this is where the magic begins.

Step 2 Crack the egg into a bowl and beat it until smooth and golden. Measure one tablespoon carefully, like you’re measuring possibility.

Step 3 In a small bowl, blend the softened butter with both sugars. Stir until creamy and pale, until it looks like something comforting and familiar.

Step 4 Add the egg and vanilla. Mix gently. The scent should start to rise soft, sweet, promising.

Step 5 In another bowl, combine flour, baking soda, and salt. Simple ingredients, quiet but essential.

Step 6 Slowly add the dry ingredients into the creamy mixture. Stir just until combined. Don’t rush it. Let it come together naturally.

Step 7 Fold in the chocolate chips. Make sure they’re scattered generously, every bite deserves a little surprise.

Step 8 Shape the dough into a soft ball and place it on the baking tray. It may look small now. Trust it.

Step 9 (Optional) Let the dough rest in the refrigerator for 15–30 minutes. Good things deepen with a little patience.

Step 10 Bake for 9–11 minutes. Watch as the edges turn golden and the center stays tender. The smell will tell you it’s almost ready.

Step 11 Remove from the oven and let it rest on the tray for 5 minutes. It will settle, finish becoming itself.

Step 12 Transfer to a rack and wait just long enough so you don’t burn your fingers. Break it open. Let the chocolate melt.

And for a moment, let everything else wait.
